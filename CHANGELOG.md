## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-27

### Added

- Terraform in two environments for two accounts: the security account's archive (a customer-managed key, a bucket with Object Lock in compliance mode, versioning and deletes denied to everyone) and the workload account's multi-region trail with log file validation, delivering to it. Detections deployed as EventBridge rules in three Regions with encrypted alert topics and email alerts, posture management (AWS Config and Security Hub), a budget, and a drill module for the deliberate, temporary weakness. Providers pinned and locked for macOS and Linux.
- Five detections as data in `detections/` (D1 new long-lived credential, D2 storage made public, D3 broad privilege grant, D4 unexpected Region, D5 honeytoken used), one source of truth for the deployed rule and the tested pattern.
- `detect_respond`: an EventBridge pattern matcher, detections, alert rendering, a timeline rebuilt from the archive alone, the time-to-detect and time-to-contain measures, containment that denies a role's older sessions and deactivates keys (a dry run by default), archive digest verification, the posture baseline with accepted risks, a plan audit that proves the deployed rules equal the files, and a blameless review generator.
- A synthetic rehearsal archive reproducing the lesson's drill (32 seconds to detect, 6 minutes 52 seconds to contain), generated by a script and checked by a test.
- Policy as code: OPA rules for the archive, the trail, the rules and targets, the drill fence, credentials and posture, with a deny and an allow test for every rule.
- The runbook scripts for both accounts, the drill and the teardown.
- A CI workflow that runs lint, tests, the credential-free plans, the rehearsal replay, the audit and the end-to-end pipeline.
